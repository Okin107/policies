# Idle Adventure: Champions of Realms — Refund Policy

All in-app purchases in Idle Adventure: Champions of Realms are sold and processed by **Apple's App Store** or **Google Play**, depending on your device. The Service Provider (Niko Goga) never receives your payment details and can't issue refunds directly — refunds follow the store's own rules.

## Asking for a refund

**iPhone and iPad (App Store)**

Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in with your Apple Account, find the purchase and choose **Request a refund**. Apple decides on every request. See [Apple's support page](https://support.apple.com/118223) for more.

**Android (Google Play)**

Open [play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory), find the purchase and choose **Request a refund**, or follow [Google Play's refund guide](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). Google Play decides on every request.

## What happens after a refund

When a store refunds a purchase, the items or currency it gave may be **removed from your account**.

## A purchase that didn't arrive

If you were charged but didn't get what you bought, contact us before asking for a refund — we can usually fix it. Send your **player ID** (Settings → Account) and the store's **order or receipt number** to **nikogoga87@gmail.com**, or reach us on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/G6ewzKfB9r).

## Deleting your account

Deleting your account doesn't refund past purchases. See [Account & Data Deletion](account-deletion.md).

This refund policy is effective as of 2026-10-08.
