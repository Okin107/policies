# Idle Adventure: Champions of Realms — Account & Data Deletion

## Delete your account in the game

Open **Settings → Account → Delete account** and confirm. Your account and all of its data are deleted from our servers straight away, and the game starts over on your device with a new, empty profile.

## Other ways to ask

If you can't open the game any more, send an email to **nikogoga87@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Delete my account"** and include your **player ID** (tap **Copy** next to it in Settings → Account) or your **player name**. If you signed in with Google or Apple, tell us which one.

You can also reach us on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/G6ewzKfB9r).

## What gets deleted

- Your player profile (player ID, player name, portrait)
- Your Google or Apple sign-in link, and any email address or name received from it
- All game progress and save data (cards, gear, currencies, progress, mail, settings)
- Leaderboard and Arena entries
- Guild membership
- Device information and device/sign-in history (device identifiers, hashed IP addresses, account events)

## What gets kept

- **Purchase records**: what was bought, when, the price and the store's transaction number. We need them for our accounts and taxes and to handle refunds. They no longer carry your player ID or name, so they can't be linked back to you.
- **An anonymous summary**: the days the account was made and deleted, the platform, game version, player level and how far you got. It has no player ID, name, email or device information.

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so their own records of a payment are kept by them under their privacy policies, not by us. Deleting your account doesn't cancel or refund past purchases.

## How long it takes

Deleting in the game is immediate. A request by email or Discord is completed within **7 days** of receiving it.
