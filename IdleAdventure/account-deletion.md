# Idle Adventure — Account & Data Deletion

## Delete your account in the game

Open **Settings**, tap the **cloud button** (Account), then **Delete account** and confirm. Your account and its data are deleted from our servers straight away, and the game starts over on your device with a new, empty profile.

## Other ways to ask

If you play as a guest or can't open the game any more, send an email to **support@phmn-solutions.com** with the subject line **"Delete my account"** and include your **Account ID** (tap **Copy** next to it in the Account window) or your **username**. If you signed in with Google or Apple, tell us which one.

You can also reach us on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/G6ewzKfB9r).

## What gets deleted

- Your account (username and password hash)
- Your Google or Apple sign-in link, and any email address received from it
- All game progress and save data, including event, season and New Void progress
- Leaderboard entries (main, event and season)
- Guild membership and Dungeon scores
- Device information

## What gets kept

- **Purchase records**: the store's transaction number, the product, when it was bought and whether it was refunded, linked to your account ID. We need them for our accounts and taxes and to handle refunds.
- **A short deletion record**: your account ID and username (a username that looks like an email address is masked), when the account was made, last played and deleted, the platform and game version, the highest stage, which sign-in providers were linked (just "Google" or "Apple"), purchase totals, and any cheating strikes or bans. It keeps refund and anti-cheat records correct and stops a deleted account from being restored by an old copy of the game. It has no save data, email address, device ID or IP address.

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so their own records of a payment are kept by them under their privacy policies, not by us. Deleting your account doesn't cancel or refund past purchases.

## How long it takes

Deleting in the game is immediate. A request by email or Discord is completed within **7 days** of receiving it.
