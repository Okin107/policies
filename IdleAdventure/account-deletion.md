# Idle Adventure — Account & Data Deletion

## How to request deletion

Send an email to **nikogoga87@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Delete my account"** and include your **in-game username**.

You can also reach us via the [IdleAdventure Discord server](https://discord.gg/cJFj8DBekf) in the support channel.

## What gets deleted

- Your account (username and password hash)
- All game progress and save data
- Leaderboard entries
- Purchase history records
- Device information

## What gets kept

Nothing. All data associated with your account is permanently deleted.

## How long it takes

Your data will be deleted within **7 days** of receiving your request.
